Reflexive Demand Simulator

Frontier-token demand is driven by a few unbounded, long-horizon tasks whose spend feeds their own revenue. Set the loop gains, drop a shock on the timeline, and watch the boom or the bust play out.

Built on Giovanni Cattani, “Nobody is talking seriously about AI demand” (X, 1 Sep 2026). Quarterly model on a rolling horizon; inputs are refreshed daily by an autonomous pipeline. All numbers are scenario estimates, not forecasts.
